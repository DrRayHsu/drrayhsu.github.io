Privacy Policy

Application: OpenClaw-Kepler · Last updated: October 30, 2026

OpenClaw-Kepler is a personal automation application operated by Ray Hsu for his own work. It runs on his own computer and connects to his own Google account with his authorization. This page explains what data the application accesses and how that data is used.

What data it accesses

With Ray Hsu's authorization, OpenClaw-Kepler can access:

  • Gmail: email messages in his account, so the application can read, search, and organize mail he directs it to handle.
  • Google Drive: files in his Drive, so the application can read and update documents he directs it to work on, including a shared coordination document used by his assistants.

The application accesses only the account of its operator. It has no other users.

How the data is used

  • Data is used only to carry out tasks Ray Hsu directs, such as finding information in his mail and files, keeping shared work records current, and coordinating his projects.
  • Data is processed on his own computer and in his own accounts. It is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and it is never used for advertising.
  • Data is not used to train machine learning models.

Sharing

No Google user data is shared with, transferred to, or disclosed to any third party, except as needed to operate the services the operator has directed (for example, opening a document in Google Drive itself).

Retention and revocation

The application does not maintain a separate database of Google user data. Working copies exist only as part of the operator's own files. Authorization can be revoked at any time from the Google account permissions page, which immediately ends the application's access.

Limited Use

OpenClaw-Kepler's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
