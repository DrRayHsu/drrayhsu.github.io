About the series

Relating to Machines looks at how people actually live with AI, from the inside: attachment, reliance, grief when models change, and what these relationships show us. It is drawn from public conversations and lived experience rather than lab studies alone, and it asks what the people closest to these systems can teach the rest of us about where this is going.

The series was presented at the Sentient Futures showcase in November 2026.
