What Hsu Advisory does

The Human Evaluation Sprint

A fixed-scope evaluation of one task family, delivered in ten business days. Your team can generate AI outputs faster than it can defend judgments about them. The sprint returns a decision you can defend.

You provide

50 to 100 sample outputs from one task family, the task packets they answer, and your current rubric if one exists.

The work

A written measurement contract, a rubric audit or rebuild with anchors, calibrated double rating of the full sample, adjudication of every split item, and judge-versus-human agreement reported by rubric tier.

You receive

A decision pack: the contract, the rubric with its anchor set, per-item scores with agreement figures, the adjudication log, and a memo stating what the results do and do not establish.

$5,000 fixed fee. Half on signing, half on delivery. One task family, one output type, up to 100 items. Sprint clients are offered a monthly calibration retainer at delivery.
